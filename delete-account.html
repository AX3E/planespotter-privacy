Delete your account

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Email ashtonberends11@gmail.com from the address used for your Bandit account.
Use the subject “Delete my Bandit account”. Include your public display name if you know it. Never send your password.

We verify the request using the account email and complete deletion within 30 days.

What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Private Firestore backups, cards, logbook entries and spotting sessions.
  • Your global leaderboard entry.
  • Squad membership, scores, profiles, sightings and shared-card records created by the account.

Data on your phone

Full photographs stay on the device where they were taken. Remove local data by uninstalling Bandit or using Android Settings → Apps → Bandit → Storage → Clear storage.

Signing out does not delete an account. Account deletion is permanent and cloud progress cannot be restored afterwards.
