Bandit for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Bandit is a game about photographing aircraft. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your camera, your location and your photos. It is short because the app collects very little.

Your photos never leave your phone.

Your location is sent to one aviation service to find aircraft near you. It is not stored by us, and it is not tied to your identity.

There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics of our own.

Everything the app remembers about you is stored on your phone, and is deleted when you uninstall it.

What the app uses, and why

Camera

The app takes a photo when you press the shutter. That photo is saved in the app's own private storage on your phone so it can appear in your Hangar.

Photos are never uploaded. Deciding whether a photo contains an aircraft happens entirely on your device, using Google's ML Kit image labeling library with a model bundled inside the app. The image is not sent anywhere.

Location

When you take a photo, and periodically while the Spot screen is open, the app uses your device's location for two purposes:

  • Finding aircraft near you. Your approximate coordinates are sent to the OpenSky Network, a public flight-tracking service, to ask which aircraft are currently in the sky around you. The request is anonymous — it carries no name, account or device identifier.
  • Naming the place a photo was taken. Your coordinates are turned into a place name (like "Airport Dr, Tullamarine") using the geocoding service built into your phone's operating system. On Android this is provided by Google.

We do not store your location on any server of ours, because we do not operate one. Your coordinates are saved with your spots on your phone only.

Aircraft identification

To turn a detected aircraft's broadcast ID (a public Mode-S hex code, which identifies an aircraft, not a person) into a type and tail number, the app queries adsbdb.com. Only the aircraft's code is sent. Nothing about you is included.

What is stored on your phone

Your cash, streak, unlocked aircraft, boosters, and the photos and details of every spot you log. This lives in the app's private storage. It is not backed up to us or synced anywhere.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Bandit deletes all of it. You can also clear it without uninstalling: Settings → Apps → Bandit → Storage → Clear storage. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete on request, because we hold nothing.

Accounts, advertising and tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Children

Bandit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
