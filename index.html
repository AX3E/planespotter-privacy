Bandit: Plane Spotter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Bandit is a game about photographing aircraft. This policy explains what the app does with your account, progress, camera, location, photographs and squad activity.

You can play without an account. If you create one, Bandit uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for sign-in, progress backup, global rankings and squads. Full photographs are not uploaded or backed up. Bandit has no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.

Signing in and game progress

Bandit remains playable signed out. Cloud backup, global rankings and squads require an account. Creating one uses the email address, password and public display name you provide. Firebase Authentication handles the password; Bandit never receives it in readable form.

When signed in, Cloud Firestore stores your display name, cards, scores, achievements, purchases made with earned in-game currency, logbook entries, spotting sessions, squad membership and related game progress so it can be restored on another device.

Global rankings are visible only to signed-in players. They contain your chosen display name, lifetime spot, cash and XP totals, recent daily totals and the score update time. They do not contain your email address, photographs, notes or location.

Camera and photographs

Bandit saves photographs in private storage on your device. Google's ML Kit image-labeling library checks for an aircraft on the device using a model bundled in the app. Photographs are not sent to ML Kit or another image-analysis service.

Full photographs stay on the device where they were taken. They are not uploaded or included in the current Firebase backup. A restored sighting may show a placeholder on another device.

Location

While the Spot screen is open, Bandit uses your location to:

  1. Find nearby aircraft through the OpenSky Network, or ADSB.lol using coordinates rounded to two decimal places if OpenSky is unavailable. These requests contain no Bandit account or device identifier.
  2. Name the place where a photograph was taken using the operating-system geocoder. Google provides this service on Android.
  3. Save the location of a logged sighting. Signed-in players include the record in their private Firebase progress backup.
  4. Check for interesting aircraft near the last location used while the app was open, if alerts are enabled. Bandit does not request background-location permission and discards that alert location after six hours.

Squads and Binder sharing

Squad members see each other's chosen display names and scores. Squad sightings include the aircraft, callsign when available, time, place name and coordinates.

Binder sharing is optional and starts off. If enabled, current squad members can see allowlisted card details such as aircraft identity, rarity, condition and capture date. Full photographs, notes, account email, balance and private backup are not shared. The current service has no cloud photo storage, so shared thumbnails are not uploaded.

Firebase Authentication processes authentication data in the United States. Cloud Firestore for Bandit is configured in Melbourne, Australia, although Google may process service data through its global infrastructure. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Security and retention

Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest. Firestore rules restrict private backups to the signed-in account and squad data to current members. Bandit keeps cloud data while the account exists. Backup revisions may retain earlier progress so restores are reliable.

Deleting data or an account

Remove local data by uninstalling Bandit or using Android Settings → Apps → Bandit → Storage → Clear storage.

To delete a Bandit account and its Firebase data, follow the account deletion instructions. You may email ashtonberends11@gmail.com from the account address with the subject “Delete my Bandit account”. Requests are completed within 30 days. Signing out does not delete an account.

Advertising, tracking and children

Bandit shows no advertising, does not sell personal information and does not track people across apps or websites. Bandit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
